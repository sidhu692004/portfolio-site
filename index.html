MilkoSys Dairy App

MilkoSys Dairy

The MilkoSys Dairy App is a digital solution designed for dairy owners to manage their daily operations efficiently. It provides features for milk collection, product management, billing, and delivery tracking in a simple and user-friendly way. Dairy owners can record milk entries with details like shift, quantity, fat, and SNF, while the system automatically calculates rates and generates digital bills. Apart from milk, owners can add and manage dairy products such as Paneer, Ghee, Curd, and Lassi, keeping proper records of stock and sales. The app also supports order management, where dairy owners can receive customer orders, process them, and assign deliveries to delivery boys (MilkoSys Hero App). Real-time synchronization with Firebase ensures transparency, accuracy, and instant updates. By replacing manual registers with a digital system, the app saves time, reduces errors, and enhances trust between dairy owners and customers.

Kotlin Firebase Android

MilkoSys App(Customer app)

MilkoSys

The MilkoSys Customer App is built to provide users with a convenient and reliable way to purchase milk and dairy products directly from nearby dairies. Through the app, customers can search and fetch local shops, explore available milk and dairy products, and add items to their cart for easy ordering. It supports both milk-based orders and additional dairy products like Paneer, Ghee, Curd, and Lassi. Customers can place orders instantly, track their order history, and choose between online payments (integrated with Razorpay) or cash on delivery, ensuring flexibility and security. The app also features a profile management system, allowing users to update personal details, and a help & support section to resolve issues quickly. With real-time synchronization via Firebase, the MilkoSys Customer App ensures smooth communication between dairies and users, making the entire process faster, transparent, and more reliable than traditional manual ordering.

Kotlin Firebase Android Razorpay

MilkoSys Hero App(Delivery Boy app)

MilkoSys Hero

MilkoSys Hero is a dedicated module within the MilkoSys Dairy App designed to simplify supplier (hero) management and ensure smooth milk collection operations. It allows dairy owners to register, manage, and monitor each supplier with complete details such as name, contact, shift, milk type, and daily contributions. With real-time data storage in Firebase, all supplier records are securely maintained and instantly accessible. The module supports automatic calculation of milk quantity, fat, SNF values, and payment rates, ensuring transparency and accuracy for both suppliers and the dairy. MilkoSys Hero also helps in tracking past transactions, generating supplier-wise digital bills, and managing payments through cash or online modes. With its simple yet powerful interface, the module reduces manual effort, eliminates errors, and improves communication between dairy owners and suppliers. Designed for efficiency, MilkoSys Hero empowers dairy businesses to maintain trust, accuracy, and long-term partnerships with their suppliers.

Kotlin Firebase Android
